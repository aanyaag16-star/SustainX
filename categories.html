Official Rulebook

Competition Categories & Scoring Rubrics

Detailed point allocations, waste benchmarks, compliance standards, and penalty criteria audited by the SustainX Green Audit Team.

Category 1 • Highest Weightage Max +5000 PTS

1. Sustainable Events

Campus fests, symposiums, conferences, and club gatherings generate the highest volume of disposable waste. Schools that enforce strict plastic-free protocols earn the highest tier of championship points.

Verified Green Event Practices:

✓ No single-use plastic bottles
✓ No plastic banners or PVC flex
✓ 100% reusable decorations
✓ Cloth/jute banners
✓ Digital invitations & QR schedules
✓ Reusable lanyard name tags
✓ Steel, glass, or compostable crockery
✓ 3-bin source segregation

Scoring Table:

Compliance Action Green Points Condition
Plastic-free event tag +5000 PTS Verified zero single-use plastic audit
Handing over plastic/waste to SustainX +2000 PTS Direct dry recyclable handover
Less amount of waste generated +1000 PTS Audit measured below campus average
Some reusable plastic used +500 PTS Durable, multi-turn utility containers
Heavy plastic usage penalty -2000 PTS Disposable bottles, PVC flex, plastic cutlery
📋 Official Event Waste Benchmark
Standees: Up to 2 allowed
Banners / Flex: Up to 1 allowed
Posters / Flyers: Up to 5 allowed
Single-use plastic: 0 for Plastic-Free tag
Category 2 • Circular Campus Max +1500 PTS

2. Reuse & Resource Sharing

True sustainability places Reduce > Reuse > Recycle in strict priority. Instead of schools repurchasing event props, stages, lab apparatus, and audio-visual equipment, inter-school sharing is recognized and incentivized.

Resource Sharing Action Points
Allocating / lending materials to another school +1500 PTS
Borrowing materials from another school +1000 PTS
Reusing materials for same school's next event +1000 PTS
Throwing reusable materials in bins (Penalty) -2000 PTS
Category 3 • Collection Drives Max +1200 PTS

3. Waste Collection Drive

Organized student-led collection of dry recyclables, e-waste, scrap paper, or spent laboratory glassware. Each tier is assessed by the SustainX Green Audit Team.

Collection Tier Green Points
High collection tier +1200 PTS
Medium collection tier +700 PTS
Low collection tier +300 PTS
No collection organized during scheduled drive -300 PTS
Mandatory Audit Requirements:
  • Minimum 2 student volunteers on duty
  • Submission of formal weigh-bridge receipt or logistics handover log
  • High-resolution photographs of collection hub
  • Proof of certified recycler routing

Category 5 • Creative Engineering Discretionary Audit Bonus

5. Innovation Bonus

Schools and student clubs are encouraged to prototype practical campus environmental engineering ideas. In accordance with competition guidelines, this category has no hardcoded ceiling; the SustainX Green Audit Committee reviews prototypes and awards tailored bonus scores.

Eligible Submission Examples:

  • ⚙️ Campus Paper Recycling Machine: Small-scale paper shredding and slurry press.
  • ☀️ Solar Phone Charging Kiosk: Off-grid student study pavilion stations.
  • 💧 Smart Rainwater Filtration: Run-off water harvesting for botanical gardens.
  • 🤖 AI-Assisted Waste Segregation: Computer vision camera for food courts.
  • 🎓 Plastic-Free Freshman Orientation: Sustainable welcome kits and zero single-use items.
💡 How to Apply: Faculty coordinators submit project briefs to the audit portal. The audit committee inspects live campus demonstrations before approving bonus entries.
