Competition Framework

How SustainX Works

A transparent, four-step campus sustainability cycle audited by student leaders and environmental faculty.

01
🌱

Step 1: Participate

Schools organize plastic-free events, resource lending exchanges, and collection drives following official SustainX guidelines.

02
🔍

Step 2: Get Verified

The SustainX Green Audit Team verifies photo evidence, volunteer logs, and weight slips before signing off on activities.

03
⚡

Step 3: Earn Green Points

Earn high rewards (up to +5000 for plastic-free initiatives) or avoid penalties (-2000 for single-use plastic violations).

04
🏆

Step 4: Climb Leaderboard

Rankings recalculate instantly. Compete for the annual SustainX Sustainability Championship Trophy and campus grants.

Scoring Rubrics

Sustainability Categories

Every activity is mapped into five standardized tracks with clear rewards and strict waste benchmarks.

Highest Weightage Up to +5000 PTS

1. Sustainable Events

Mandatory zero single-use plastic bottles, cloth flex banners, and proper food waste segregation during campus festivals.

Benchmark: 0 single-use plastics | Max 2 standees
Circular Sharing Up to +1500 PTS

2. Reuse & Resource Sharing

Inter-school lending of stage structures, lab kits, and exhibition fixtures to prevent wasteful single-event purchases.

Lending: +1500 | Borrowing: +1000 | Reusing: +1000
Collection Drives Up to +1200 PTS

3. Waste Collection Drive

High, medium, and low collection tiers assessed by the audit team with certified weight slips and min 2 volunteers.

High tier: +1200 | Medium: +700 | Low: +300
Student Engagement Up to +5500 PTS

4. Sustainability Participation

Student turnout in case competitions, quizzes, clean-energy hackathons, and tree plantation drives with winning team bonuses.

>30% Turnout: +3000 | Winner Bonus: +2500
Audit Committee Discretion Custom Bonus

5. Innovation Bonus

Practical student prototypes: solar charging stations, paper recycling machines, AI-assisted sorting bins, and smart rainwater harvesting.

Audited dynamically and approved individually by the SustainX Green Audit Committee.
