Championship Vision

About SustainX

The premier inter-school sustainability championship transforming universities into zero-waste, eco-conscious learning ecosystems.

The Initiative

What is SustainX?

SUSTAINX – Green Campus Challenge is an inter-school university championship created to institutionalize environmental accountability. Rather than treating sustainability as a one-day ceremonial event, SustainX introduces a continuous, competitive, and audited framework where academic schools and departments actively compete to minimize carbon and plastic footprints.

From high-energy college festivals to daily lab operations, SustainX gamifies environmental stewardship with real-time rankings, transparent audit trails, and student leadership empowerment.

The Urgency

Why Sustainability Matters

Higher education campuses are micro-cities. With thousands of students, faculty, and daily visitors, universities generate immense quantities of single-use plastic waste, discarded flex banners, paper reams, and electronic scrap.

🛑

Single-Use Crisis

Festivals routinely produce thousands of disposable plastic cups and vinyl promotional banners destined for landfills.

🔄

Resource Duplication

Individual departments repeatedly buy stage assets and lab gear that could be shared circularly.

🌱

Habit Formation

Students entering professional careers carry sustainable, circular thinking into global industries.

Operations

How the Competition Works

Participating schools earn Green Points across the academic year across five distinct categories:

  1. Sustainable Events: Zero plastic bottles, cloth flex banners, and low waste generation (+5000 max).
  2. Reuse & Resource Sharing: Inter-school equipment lending and material reuse (+1500 max).
  3. Waste Collection Drive: High, medium, and low collection drives audited by weigh-slips (+1200 max).
  4. Sustainability Participation: Student engagement in clean energy quizzes and hackathons (+5500 max).
  5. Innovation Bonus: Student prototypes approved by the Green Audit Committee.
Integrity & Auditing

Rigorous Green Auditing

To ensure fairness, normal users and students cannot self-assign scores. Every score entry must pass through the SustainX Green Audit Team:

  • ✓ Evidence Verification: Weigh-slips from recyclers, event photographs, and volunteer timesheets must be submitted.
  • ✓ Unannounced Inspections: Audit committee members perform surprise spot checks at major campus fests.
  • ⚠ Strict Penalties: Single-use plastic violations incur immediate -2000 Green Point reductions on the official leaderboard.
  • ✓ Approved Status Only: Only audit entries with verificationStatus: 'approved' contribute to a school's leaderboard score.
